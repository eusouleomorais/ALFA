# ALFA Documentation

## Mission

ALFA is a Decision Intelligence Engine designed to support trading decisions through the integration of technical analysis, macroeconomic information, statistical evidence, and risk management.

Its objective is not to predict the future with certainty, but to estimate the probability of the most likely market scenarios and recommend:

- BUY
- SELL
- WAIT

Every recommendation must be explainable, evidence-based, statistically supported, and accompanied by a confidence score.

---

## Core Principles

- Evidence over opinion
- Statistical validation
- Risk first
- Explainable decisions
- Continuous improvement
